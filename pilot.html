Who it is for

Teams that need a decision process they can inspect.

For organizations evaluating an AI system, testing a forecast process, assuring a research conclusion, or structuring a consequential decision with a documented evidence trail.

Use cases: AI evaluation · forecasting · research assurance · decision intelligence.

What you provide

A narrow question and the controls for a fair test.

  • One tightly defined evaluation scope and question set
  • Relevant evidence sources, permitted information boundaries, and a named evaluation owner
  • Existing acceptance criteria, comparators, or decision gates, if applicable
  • Agreement on cutoff dates, scoring, and handling of results before execution

What KAHRELUM does

Freeze the method, test it, preserve the record.

KAHRELUM helps define the scope, freezes one protocol before outputs exist, records evidence separately from inference, challenges the conclusion with a countercase, and reproduces the evaluation within the agreed scope.

The evaluation may be scored or structured, depending on the question and available resolution criteria.

What you receive

A report and a usable audit trail.

  • Frozen scope and protocol
  • Evidence and inference record
  • Adversarial challenge and countercase
  • Scored or structured evaluation, where applicable
  • One final decision/evaluation report
  • Limitations, failure modes, and preserved negative or inconclusive findings
  • One readout call

Timeline

Target delivery: 5 business days.

The target window begins after scope and protocol freeze. Timing may be revised if agreed inputs or dependencies are delayed.

Price

Founding pilot: $2,500 fixed.

Includes one tightly defined evaluation scope, one frozen protocol, one final decision/evaluation report, and one readout call. Expanded or custom scopes are quoted separately.

Limitations

Results stay bounded by the tested scope.

No guaranteed result and no claim of universal superiority. Findings apply only to the agreed scope and conditions. Negative and inconclusive results remain in the record. KAHRELUM's published retrospective benchmark has limitations; it does not establish commercial superiority. No pilot engagement is implied by this offer.
