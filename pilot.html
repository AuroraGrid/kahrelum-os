Private Pilot / Prospective Validation

Test the decision process before trusting the answer.

KAHRELUM is seeking externally controlled evaluations for forecasting, decision intelligence, AI evaluation, and research assurance. A credible pilot freezes the questions, evidence cutoffs, scoring, and success criteria before execution—and preserves negative or inconclusive results.

Potential problem types

Choose a question where the evidence can be inspected.

Forecasting

Unresolved questions with defined horizons, resolution criteria, and proper scoring such as Brier score where appropriate.

Decision intelligence

Evidence-to-decision cases that need alternatives, explicit falsifiers, and action gates.

AI evaluation

Frozen side-by-side evaluation against existing systems under a declared protocol.

Research assurance

Test whether unsupported or incomplete conclusions are blocked before release.

How a pilot works

Controls are agreed before outputs exist.

  1. Scope. Define the question set, intended decision use, information boundary, and evaluation owner.
  2. Set controls. Preferably, the external evaluator controls case selection, cutoff dates, preregistration, resolution criteria, comparators, scoring, and preservation or publication of results.
  3. Freeze the protocol. Record scoring rules, success criteria, and handling procedures before execution.
  4. Execute the authorized scope. Preserve blocks, refusals, deviations, and negative findings. Any data handling must be agreed before sensitive material is shared.
  5. Report the result. Interpret findings only within the tested benchmark and its conditions.

This proposed process follows the published prospective-validation protocol. It does not imply that a pilot has already run.

Expected deliverables

A record that another evaluator can inspect.

Per-case record

Frozen question and cutoff; output and probability when required; evidence and block/refusal state; resolution and score.

Aggregate report

Preregistered score, calibration, paired comparisons and uncertainty intervals where appropriate, deviations, and failure-mode analysis. Cost or runtime appears only if measured.

Limitations

Evidence stays bounded by the test.

A prospective result is evidence about its defined benchmark and conditions. It does not establish universal or commercial superiority, third-party certification, or guaranteed decision outcomes. Negative and inconclusive results remain part of the record. No price, customer, or result is implied here.
