# KAHRELUM Commercial Positioning — AI Agent Decision Assurance

**Status:** Active commercial focus (as of 2026-11-23)

## Core product

Independent, inspectable review of historical AI agent decisions against the evidence, authority, and decision rules that existed at the time of the action.

Deliverable: structured case record (Brief / Evidence / Inference / Uncertainty / Counterargument / Evaluation / Limitations / Record) plus FIT / NO-FIT summary with explicit gaps and negative findings.

## Published entry points

- **$750 Fit Check** — one workflow, three de-identified historical decisions, one-page support-gap findings.
- **$2,500 Founding Pilot** — one workflow, up to ten decisions, individual case records, consolidated analysis, readout call.
- Full $750 credited toward a pilot started within 14 days.

All work remains operator-assisted until the self-serve upload path is live. No production access, no certification claim, no performance guarantee.

## What is not on the primary commercial surface

Sports Intelligence Desk, election research, investigations, forecasting calibration public pages, and the KAHRELUM ONE concept live under **Lab**. They demonstrate the underlying evidence-to-decision method and remain publicly available; they are not the commercial entry point.

## Buyer problem (one sentence)

When an AI agent recommends, approves, blocks, or escalates, did the contemporaneous evidence and the applicable rules actually justify that choice?

## Next product step

Self-serve upload of de-identified decision packages that runs a constrained version of the existing pipeline and returns the same inspectable record format.
