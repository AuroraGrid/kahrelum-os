Forecast accountability

KAHRELUM's October 31 model research and earlier archived predictions are not changed by an election result. We assess matched outcomes only after verifying the original forecast's cutoff, event wording, candidate or caucus mapping and applicable resolution rules. Historical confidence cannot be reassigned after the vote.
