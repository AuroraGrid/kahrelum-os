KAHRELUM / enterprise assurance

Test the evidence behind high-stakes AI decisions.

KAHRELUM evaluates whether an AI-assisted decision was supportable from the evidence, rules and authority available when it was made. The present service is an operator-assisted historical review. Automated vendor comparison and continuous governance are product-development directions, not currently available hosted services.

Current commercial service

What an initial review actually delivers

Decision evidence reconstruction

Freeze the review cutoff, identify source claims and policy authority, separate known facts from inference, and preserve absent evidence.

Independent countercheck

Examine alternative explanations and unsupported steps. Issue a case-specific FIT, NO FIT or indeterminate finding within the agreed protocol, rather than implying universal model reliability.

Traceable output

Written case records with decision context, evidence citations when available, explicit gaps, next evidence required and a consolidated readout for eligible packages.

Controlled intake

Confirm permission, supplier eligibility, processing jurisdiction and handling arrangements before any client material is transferred. No confidential records through the public inquiry.

Future product architecture / not available now

Five workflows, one assessment method

Clinical note accuracy, clinician-inbox escalation, patient-context evidence grounding, editorial citation fidelity and automated laboratory validation can all be examined through an evidence-to-decision record. Relevance does not prove we have tested any named vendor's systems.
