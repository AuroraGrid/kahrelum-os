# KAHRELUM | AI Assurance, Investigations & Forecasting

Canonical page: https://kahrelum.com/
Language: English.

KAHRELUM is an evidence-to-decision research and retrospective AI decision-assurance practice. It preserves what was available at the decision cutoff, separates fact from inference, challenges conclusions and records unresolved uncertainty rather than declaring unwarranted certainty.

## Fixed-scope commercial entry

KAHRELUM offers a $750 upfront Fit Check for exactly three permissioned, de-identified closed historical decisions in one workflow, with a one-page support-gap record and FIT/NO FIT finding. Target two business days after scope, permitted inputs and cleared payment. Full $750 credit toward a $2,500 Founding Pilot initiated within 14 days.

The $2,500 upfront Founding Pilot covers one workflow and up to ten authorized closed historical decisions, individual records, consolidated findings and a readout call. Target five business days after scope/protocol freeze, permitted inputs and payment.

No production access, certification, guaranteed performance lift, customer outcomes or direct website checkout are claimed. See https://kahrelum.com/pilot.
