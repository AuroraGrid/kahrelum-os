# Request a KAHRELUM Pilot

**Current status: Private Pilot / Prospective Validation.**

KAHRELUM is looking for organizations willing to test it on externally controlled forecasting, decision-intelligence, AI-evaluation or research-assurance problems.

A strong pilot freezes the questions, cutoffs, scoring and success criteria before execution. The partner may control the evaluation completely.

## Suggested pilot shapes

- forecasting: unresolved questions with proper scoring and calibration;
- decision intelligence: evidence-to-decision cases with explicit falsifiers and action gates;
- AI evaluation: frozen side-by-side evaluation against existing systems;
- research assurance: test whether unsupported or incomplete conclusions are blocked before release.

## Start

For an initial conversation, email [hasan@kahrelum.com](mailto:hasan@kahrelum.com) with a short, non-confidential description of the problem type and the kind of evaluation you have in mind. The link opens an email draft; it does not submit a request automatically. Do not put confidential, proprietary or personal information in a public GitHub issue. Do not send sensitive material by email until an appropriate handling process has been agreed.

## Process and expected outputs

1. Scope the question set, decision use, evidence boundary, and evaluation owner.
2. Agree who controls case selection, cutoffs, preregistration, resolution criteria, comparators, scoring, and preservation of results. The preferred protocol gives those controls to the external evaluator.
3. Freeze the protocol and success criteria before any execution. No test outcome or performance result is promised.
4. If both sides authorize the work and its data handling, execute only the agreed scope and preserve blocks, refusals, deviations, and negative findings.
5. Deliver a case-level record (question, cutoff, output, probability when required, evidence and block state, resolution, score) plus an aggregate report (the preregistered score, calibration, paired comparisons and uncertainty intervals where appropriate, and failure-mode analysis). Cost or runtime is reported only if measured.

These are proposed pilot steps and deliverables based on the [public prospective-validation protocol](evidence/PROSPECTIVE_VALIDATION_PROTOCOL.md); they are not evidence that a pilot has already run.

## Limitations

A prospective result supports conclusions only for its defined benchmark and conditions. It does not establish universal or commercial superiority, third-party certification, or guaranteed decision outcomes. Negative and inconclusive results remain part of the record. No price, customer, or result is implied by this page.
