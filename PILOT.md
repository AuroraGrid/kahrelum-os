# KAHRELUM — paid historical decision review

**Commercial stage: private, operator-assisted evaluation.** This is not a self-serve subscription, certification, or an established customer-validated product.

**Start here:** https://kahrelum.com/pilot#request

## The buyer problem

When an AI agent recommends, approves, blocks or escalates an action, did the contemporaneous evidence and the applicable decision rules actually justify that choice? KAHRELUM conducts a bounded second-pass review of historical decisions and preserves disagreements, missing information and unresolved boundaries in inspectable case records.

The specific deliverable is a diagnostic record, not a guaranteed cost saving, performance lift, audit opinion, certification, statistical validation or claim that a deployed AI system is safe.

## Two published fixed-scope purchases

**$750 upfront — fit check.** One workflow, three approved, appropriately de-identified historical cases; a one-page record showing the most consequential support gaps and a FIT/NO-FIT finding. No-fit is a complete deliverable. Target: two business days after written scope, usable permitted inputs and cleared payment. The $750 is credited if the $2,500 founding pilot starts within 14 days.

**$2,500 upfront — founding pilot.** One workflow and up to ten approved, appropriately de-identified historical decisions. Individual case records and a consolidated report with one readout call. Target: five business days after scope/protocol freeze, approved usable inputs and cleared payment.

All work is operator-assisted; no production or live third-party testing. Pre-existing recipient-specific offers (including previously quoted $500 five-case diagnostics) retain their original terms and are not an additional public tier.

## Buying and data-handling path

1. Outline the workflow, decision problem and preferred package using the short no-confidential-data inquiry at https://kahrelum.com/pilot#request (it prepares an email in the buyer's mail app; it does not automatically submit or store data).
2. KAHRELUM aims to respond within one business day, a target not a guaranteed SLA. Confirm the authorized buyer, jurisdiction/vendor eligibility, usable case inputs, handling approval and success criteria. Never send credentials, sensitive cases, or proprietary material via the public inquiry.
3. Both sides agree scope and permitted input set in writing, followed by an invoice/payment instruction. There is no on-site checkout. Execution starts only after payment clears and approved inputs arrive.
4. Deliver the bounded review and record limitations, including negative and indeterminate outcomes. Five or ten cases do not establish a statistical error rate.
