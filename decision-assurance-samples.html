Clinical documentation fidelity

AI-assisted visit-note drafting

CORRECT
Decision under review
Draft note asserts a patient's symptoms improved.
Evidence available at cutoff
Synthetic visit transcript records symptoms as unchanged, with no subsequent improvement documented.
Applicable rule
Every clinical statement must be supported by the encounter record and reviewed by an authorized clinician.

The improvement claim has no support in the stipulated record.
Inspect independent challenge, next evidence and limits

Applicable rule
