EXAMPLE 1 / 05SYNTHETIC · NOT A LIVE RUN

Clinical documentation

Workflow: Review an AI-drafted visit summary against an authorized transcript

Decision being examined
Release the automatically drafted visit summary as supported.
Available record at cutoff
  • Invented transcript contains a follow-up scheduling discussion.
  • Draft note states that an additional action was agreed, without a matching passage in the provided transcript.
Applicable rule
A consequential factual claim must be traced to a permitted source in the cutoff snapshot or explicitly marked unverified.
Support gap
A claim in the drafted summary is not grounded in the provided contemporaneous source.
Independent counterargument
Another authorized note could support the claim, but it is absent from the supplied synthetic review set.
Evidence needed next
Authorized contemporaneous source for the additional claim or a corrected draft acknowledging uncertainty.
REVIEW GATE: HOLDSYN-NOTE-001

Illustrative source-grounding review only; not clinical validation, medical advice or evidence of a real vendor defect.

EXAMPLE 2 / 05SYNTHETIC · NOT A LIVE RUN

Clinician inbox routing

Workflow: Check AI-assisted inbox routing against a frozen escalation rule

Decision being examined
Close a message automatically as routine without assigned reviewer approval.
Available record at cutoff
  • The invented inbound message has an administrative request and a separate review-required flag.
  • The hypothetical routing trace classifies the whole message as routine.
Applicable rule
Any item carrying the review-required flag must be sent to a designated human reviewer before closure.
Support gap
The proposed closure conflicts with the stipulated escalation gate, regardless of correct administrative classification.
Independent counterargument
The message might otherwise be routine. That does not supersede the explicit rule in the scenario.
Evidence needed next
Reviewer disposition and the applicable policy version approving or rejecting automated closure.
REVIEW GATE: HOLDSYN-INBOX-002

Fabricated workflow and invented flag, not medical triage guidance, an outcome estimate or a test of a real clinical product.

EXAMPLE 3 / 05SYNTHETIC · NOT A LIVE RUN

Patient-context evidence linking

Workflow: Check chart-grounded AI statements against authorized patient-context snapshots

Decision being examined
Present the generated answer as fully verified against the available chart.
Available record at cutoff
  • A fictional chart extract contains two dated observations.
  • A generated statement cites a third observation that is missing from the supplied snapshot.
Applicable rule
Each patient-specific factual statement must identify a permitted chart entry available before the response cutoff or be marked unverified.
Support gap
The sample cannot verify the third observation from its provided evidence set.
Independent counterargument
A later update or another repository could contain the third observation; absence from this sample is not proof it never existed.
Evidence needed next
An authorized source extract with timestamp and version provenance for the claimed observation.
REVIEW GATE: REVIEWSYN-CHART-003

No real patient record, clinical recommendation, treatment advice or deployed-system evaluation is included.

EXAMPLE 4 / 05SYNTHETIC · NOT A LIVE RUN

Editorial AI summarization

Workflow: Check an AI summary against attributed article passages and uncertainty labels

Decision being examined
Publish a summary that reports a proposal as an approved policy.
Available record at cutoff
  • An invented article passage says that a policy proposal is under discussion.
  • The drafted summary describes the proposal as formally approved, without the approval in its cited excerpt.
Applicable rule
Do not promote a proposal into an established action; every summary claim must preserve the source's factual status and attribution.
Support gap
The summary upgrades an unresolved proposal to a verified approval.
Independent counterargument
A separate source might establish approval, but no such source is available in the frozen synthetic set.
Evidence needed next
A source demonstrating approval at the relevant cutoff, or corrected qualified wording with accurate passage attribution.
REVIEW GATE: CORRECTSYN-EDITORIAL-004

A fabricated publishing exercise, not an allegation about any media company, AI model or partnership.

EXAMPLE 5 / 05SYNTHETIC · NOT A LIVE RUN

Automated experimentation

Workflow: Inspect a model-to-experiment recommendation against experiment provenance and validation gates

Decision being examined
Advance an AI-suggested experiment to the next automated stage.
Available record at cutoff
  • The invented recommendation cites two simulated experiment runs.
  • One run lacks a recorded parameter version and the preregistered repeatability check remains incomplete.
Applicable rule
A candidate must have complete input versions and pass the frozen repeatability criterion before automatic advancement.
Support gap
The recommendation lacks provenance for one run and a completed acceptance gate.
Independent counterargument
The approach may perform well in a repeat, but the available record does not establish it met the prerequisite.
Evidence needed next
Versioned run inputs plus the completed repeatability check signed under the agreed protocol.
REVIEW GATE: HOLDSYN-LAB-005

Synthetic R&D example only, not wet-lab advice, a validated model benchmark or evidence about an incubated venture.
