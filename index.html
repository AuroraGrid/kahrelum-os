What an output looks like

Not just an answer — an inspectable decision state.

QUESTION
Will a defined event occur before the frozen horizon?

FACT
Verified evidence available at the declared cutoff.

INFERENCE
Mechanism connecting that evidence to the event.

FORECAST
62% — example format only, not a live forecast.

BLACKGLASS
Strongest countercase and evidence that would invalidate the thesis.

COMMAND
MONITOR — no action until the declared gate clears.

BLOCK CONDITION
If the required evidence object cannot be verified, do not manufacture closure.

Israel 2026 election: C7 completed

Case KAH-IL26-PROSPECTIVE-001 · C7 completed 5 November 2026 · original stages preserved

The Israeli Central Elections Committee signed the final 26th Knesset results on November 4: 120 seats, 5,691,426 valid votes, with Likud the largest list on 27 seats. KAHRELUM's C7 record compares the official outcome with its externally attributed frozen October 26 fourteen-list forecast: L1 20 seats, MAE 1.4286, RMSE 2.0702.

Ten preregistered process gates: 6 PASS / 4 MISS. C1 was 13m23s late; the 25%, 50% and 75% valid-vote counting checkpoints were missed and captured simultaneously after the full provisional count. The October 27 KAHRELUM seat estimate was conditioned on exit polls after voting closed, so it is not a pre-election forecast victory. No proprietary party/winner probability was prospectively frozen; its election-outcome Brier is not evaluable.
