How the forecast was made

One million seeded simulations cover 28 remaining NFC East-relevant games. Shared matchups are simulated only once. The model begins with the four teams' Nov 12 standings, Pete Ratings' neutral-field strength estimates through Nov 9, a disclosed 1.1-point home advantage, and an uncalibrated seven-point logistic margin-to-probability conversion. Two near-term games use disclosed market-anchored win probabilities.

Limitations: This is an independent experimental model, not a certified or backtested KAHRELUM forecasting engine. Future ties, major personnel changes and evolving injuries are not modeled. A rare deep tiebreak class (349 simulations) is evenly allocated. Rating uncertainty dominates the small Monte Carlo sampling error.

Shifting the Giants' underlying strength by two points changes their title chance to about 55.8% or 75.8%. Treat 66.4% as a scenario-based estimate, not a uniquely precise truth.
