02 · Procedure and sensitivity

Majority arithmetic is not government formation

Model deterministic what-if departures from full party attendance. The adjustments below are applied to simulated parliamentary votes only. They do not represent estimates of absent members, defections, legal approvals, negotiations or future conduct.

This tool distinguishes absolute majority and yes-versus-no arithmetic; it is not a legal finding.

For actual procedure and vote standards consult the Knesset and Israel Democracy Institute. Constructive no-confidence is distinct from a hypothetical investiture vote.
