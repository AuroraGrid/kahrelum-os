The frozen KAHRELUM distribution

Our model was selected before deadline day by a five-year historical walk-forward comparison. The simpler historical Poisson benchmark outperformed our more complex alternative. Eight NFL deadline seasons (2018–2025) contributed 52 uniquely counted deadline-day trades, giving an expected count of 6.5. These are research-method facts, not evidence of superiority over insiders.

Event definition

Number of distinct player-involving trade transactions recorded in the NFL's November 2026 trades register with an effective date of Nov 10 and executed by 4 PM ET. One exchange counts once, even if multiple players or picks move.

Trades registered for Nov 9 are excluded from this count. A reported agreement is not automatically scored as an officially completed transaction.
