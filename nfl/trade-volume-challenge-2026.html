Event contract

What counts as one trade?

A distinct player-involving trade transaction, recorded with a November 10 effective transaction date and executed by 4 p.m. Eastern. A two-team exchange counts once, regardless of multiple players or draft picks.

Monday trades and reported-but-uncompleted agreements do not count as Tuesday trades. Missing or delayed registry entries require reconciliation, not an automatic zero.

Audit protocol and eventual scoring
