PROTOCOL 02

Film packet admission

01 / Source

Permitted viewing basis

Official league/team highlights, licensed access, or user-authorized media. The public page stores a link and hashable notes, not the protected video.

02 / Context

Game, quarter, clock, down

Every observation identifies the play context. A montage without the surrounding sample cannot establish a rate or consistent trait.

03 / Review

Observation before conclusion

Technique, assignment and result are recorded separately. Unknown assignment remains unknown.

04 / Publication

Editor gate

Only reviewed notes may be summarized. Grades disclose sample size, excluded plays and uncertainty.

05 / Revision

Original note preserved

Corrections append a new timestamp and reason. Earlier observations are not silently rewritten.

06 / Forecast boundary

Film is not a trade probability

Play evaluation may inform analysis but never creates a forecast without an explicit event, rationale and prospective lock.

Rights boundary: KAHRELUM does not bypass NFL+, paywalls, authentication, geographic restrictions or platform controls. No downloaded or mirrored game footage is included in this release.
