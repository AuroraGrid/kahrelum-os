# KAHRELUM Historical AI Decision Review | Fixed-Scope Pilot

Canonical page: https://kahrelum.com/pilot

## What is evaluated
KAHRELUM independently and retrospectively checks whether the evidence, rules, cutoff information, approvals and escalation authority available before an AI-assisted decision justified that decision. Findings may be FIT, NO FIT or indeterminate; negative results are valid deliverables. This is an operator-assisted specialist review, not production monitoring, certification or statistically established system safety.

## $750 Fit Check
One agreed workflow and exactly three authorized, appropriately de-identified, closed historical decisions. One-page evidence-to-decision record showing the most consequential support gaps, uncertainties and FIT/NO FIT. Payment: $750 upfront. Delivery target: two business days after agreed written scope, usable permitted inputs and cleared payment.

## $2,500 Founding Pilot
One agreed workflow, up to ten authorized, appropriately de-identified historical decisions, per-case records, consolidated findings and a readout call. Payment: $2,500 upfront. Delivery target: five business days after scope/protocol freeze, usable inputs and cleared payment.

The $750 Fit Check fee is fully credited toward a $2,500 Founding Pilot begun within 14 days. Prior recipient-specific quotes retain their original terms.
