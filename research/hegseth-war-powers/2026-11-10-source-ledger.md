## Claim ledger and residual uncertainties

- Thread claim: Every President objected to WPR => WPR is wholly unconstitutional and legally nonbinding.
  Assessment: unsupported leap from executive position to conclusive legal status; distinction between contested provisions mandatory.
- Thread claim: hundreds of deployments and 11 declarations establish executive-only right.
  Assessment: false binary; CRS R42738 explicitly denies legality inference and records other congressional authorizations.
- Thread claim: no binding statutory civilian-harm framework for DoD.
  Assessment: overbroad in light of Title 10 §184; individual actions/causation unresolved.
- Thread claim: retired military jurisdiction eliminates Kelly free-speech legal concern.
  Assessment: federal preliminary injunction and First Amendment analysis constitute contrary evidence; appellate merits outcome not independently verified.
- Thread claim: NIAC determination automatically makes alleged drug-boat traffickers targetable.
  Assessment: NIAC and DPH require legally relevant facts under IHL; verification of each incident and legal theory remains open.
- Thread claim: Maduro's nonrecognition proves Venezuelan sovereignty not implicated.
  Assessment: invalid categorical inference; operation-specific UN Charter and international law questions remain.
- Thread claim: impeachment articles are conclusively sham.
  Assessment: a partisan advocacy conclusion, not a demonstrated judicial finding. Separate legal strength from House vote probability.
