**Headline: Giants' Taylor pursuit meets an uncertain Colts price as NFL deadline nears**

**FACT:** The NFL's November 2026 trade register did not list a November transaction when checked this afternoon; Arizona QB Jacoby Brissett's reported move to Baltimore is still awaiting documented official execution. The NFL trade deadline is Tuesday, Nov. 10, at 4 p.m. New York time. Sources: https://www.nfl.com/transactions/league/trades/2026/11 ; https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-important-dates .

**REPORT:** In FOX Sports original reporting updated Nov. 5 at 1:49 p.m. Eastern, Ralph Vacchiano says the New York Giants remain highly interested in Indianapolis running back Jonathan Taylor. He cites an executive's uncertainty over how willing the Colts are to trade Taylor at the reported price. One source told Vacchiano they had *heard* Indianapolis wanted a first-round pick included. That does **not** independently establish a formal Colts asking price or prove the Giants submitted a first-round offer. Coauthor Eric D. Williams favors Minnesota as Taylor's eventual landing spot, illustrating that even the reporters' own predictions diverge. Original: https://www.foxsports.com/stories/nfl/nfl-trade-deadline-rumors-predictions-jonathan-taylor-josh-downs-brian-thomas .

**CONTRARY EVIDENCE:** NFL Network's Ian Rapoport reported during Nov. 5 Good Morning Football that the Colts were targeting a draft return comparable to Christian McCaffrey's 2022 trade package, historically second, third, fourth and fifth-round selections. Separately Jordan Schultz's original Nov. 5 attribution says Indianapolis may wait until after Sunday's Cowboys game before revisiting offers, barring an exceptional earlier proposal; his original X post is identified but direct access was blocked. These are separate perspectives on negotiations, not proof of a revised official price. Date-attributed relay for Rapoport: https://nfltraderumors.co/teams-arent-meeting-colts-asking-price-for-jonathan-taylor-josh-downs-yet/ . Schultz original URL: https://x.com/Schultz_Report/status/2118356386266460577 ; dated embedded quotation: https://denversports.com/nfl/denver-broncos/if-the-broncos-pull-off-a-jonathan-taylor-trade-it-may-have-to-wait-until-after-sunday .

**INFERENCE:** The newly described Giants commitment supports the buyer side of the market, but unsettled draft compensation and Indianapolis' preference to retain a core player still weigh against a quick deal. A Taylor trade would change backfield opportunity allocation, but no fantasy adjustment belongs on a trade rumor.
