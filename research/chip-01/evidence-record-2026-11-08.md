## Core question
What can be authenticated about corporate control, financing, purchase licenses, chip serial-number custody, data-center installation, cloud end users, and possible government intelligence dissemination? What would disprove each allegation?

## Evidence distinctions
1. **FACT (primary):** U.S. congressional committee chairs asked Nvidia's Jensen Huang to brief them November 18, with a November 11 response deadline (Nov 5 announcement).
2. **FACT (primary):** Singapore MHA on Oct 31 denied investigating Alice Huang's alleged Chinese intelligence activities while confirming ongoing investigations into Megaspeed.
3. **REPORTED allegation:** FT on Nov 7 described an intelligence-based U.S. memo alleging Alice Huang was recruited by China's MSS and claimed a private relationship with Jensen Huang. Underlying classified records are unseen.
4. **REPORTED:** Bloomberg's Dec 2025/Jan 2026 reporting indicated at least 136,000 GPUs imported through Nov 2025 and ~86,000 identified at certain data centers. Different accounting bases; the delta is NOT evidence of missing GPUs.
5. **REPORTED:** NYT Oct 29 (syndicated) said Commerce referred an inquiry to DOJ. No public charging document or referral original authenticated here.
6. **FACT (regulatory):** BIS's May 2026 guidance clarifies D:5 ultimate-parent and headquarters-linked requirements for specified advanced computing products overseas. Jan 2026 H200 rule is conditional license review, not blanket authorization.
7. **CONTRARY:** Nvidia says interactions were routine and its checks found no diversion. Singapore rejects purported Singapore intelligence investigation.
8. **UNRELATED:** DOJ March 19 announced a separate alleged server-diversion case involving staged dummy servers. No documented link to Megaspeed.

## Falsification and next evidence
- Certified corporate registry, parent/control agreements, financing attestations.
- Serialized shipments matched to customs, physical facilities and authorized end users.
- Nvidia NCP partner-screening and Fleet Intelligence obligations for current partners.
- Dated agency dissemination list, export license decisions and DOJ referral confirmation.
- Signed response from parties and dated congressional briefing follow-up.
