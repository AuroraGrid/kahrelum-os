## Competing hypotheses, falsifiers and constraints

1. **Foreign-intelligence targeting without proven compromise:** A documented targeting attempt can coexist with no disclosed secret transfer. Test with independent board findings, confirmed law-enforcement disclosures, communications chronology, access logs and protective controls.
2. **Broader information or disclosure failure:** A materially documented leakage, failure to report or falsified disclosure would weaken the no-compromise position. Test against authenticated board findings, relevant law-enforcement statements, SEC filings and court outcomes, not anonymous speculation alone.
3. **Separate GPU export-diversion concern:** Chip custody, export-control licensing and ultimate ownership require serial-by-serial reconciliation, not subtraction of Bloomberg's incompatible inventory samples. No established link to Huang's private relationship may be assumed.
4. **No wrongdoing established:** The board can complete the review without substantiated compromise or breach. A clean audited chain of custody and no adverse official findings would weaken more serious theories.
