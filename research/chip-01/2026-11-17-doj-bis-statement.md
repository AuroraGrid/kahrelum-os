## What the two departments publicly state

1. The Commerce Department began investigating Megaspeed **in 2024** for possible export-control violations after proactive monitoring of potential sensitive-semiconductor diversion.
2. In **May 2025**, Commerce investigators requested records from Nvidia concerning direct or indirect sales to Megaspeed.
3. Nvidia placed Megaspeed on its **no-sell list in June 2025**, according to the joint statement.
4. BIS and Singaporean law enforcement carried out an **on-site, in-person end-use check at Megaspeed in June 2025** and interviewed then-CEO Alice Huang.
5. The Department of Justice **subsequently took the lead role in the criminal investigation** of Megaspeed.

The government announcement directly supports this investigative chronology; it does **not** include an adjudicated Megaspeed export-control violation, establish unit-by-unit chip diversion, identify a specific charging instrument against Megaspeed or its executives, or substantiate the separate allegations of espionage or personal influence concerning Jensen Huang.
