# KAHRELUM — retrospective AI decision assurance

For teams operating AI-assisted shopping, pricing or research workflows: were the evidence and rules available at the time sufficient to support a closed historical decision?

One workflow. Exactly three authorized, appropriately de-identified closed historical decisions. One-page support-gap record and FIT/NO FIT outcome. Target two business days after written scope, permitted inputs and cleared payment. Operator-assisted review, no production access, certification or guaranteed improvement.

## $2,500 upfront Founding Pilot

One workflow. Up to ten authorized, de-identified historical decisions. Per-case record, consolidated report and one readout. ## Inputs and output

Supply only permitted historical inputs, the decision, its timestamp/cutoff, applicable rules, sources available then and a clear authorized question. Exclude credentials, unnecessary personal data and confidential data outside the agreed permission scope. Outcomes may be retained separately, never used to rewrite the original evidence state. KAHRELUM identifies missing support, contradictions, correction history and when clarification/HOLD would have been appropriate.
