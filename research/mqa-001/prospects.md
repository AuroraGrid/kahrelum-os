# Prospect qualification — review only

15 unique company-level relevance-qualified candidates: five requested comparators and ten additional specialist companies. This means public product relevance and current operating surface were checked. It does NOT mean sales-qualified buyers, budget approval, permissioned cases or willingness to purchase. Those counts remain zero. Ten additions are specialist alternatives to the original enterprise cohort; exact current employee counts/company-size ordering are not established except Zipchat's self-reported 18-person team. Do not call all ten verified smaller by headcount.

Ranking is a qualitative analyst inference for a $750 three-case retrospective Fit Check, emphasizing an identifiable workflow, bounded evidence needs and practical routing. Current public pages show operating products; this is not a solvency or corporate-registration audit. No observed product defect is alleged. All evaluation requirements below are proposed scoping hypotheses, not verified internal procurement requirements.

Source retrieval method: source-retrievals*.json and zipchat-retrieval.json record direct request timestamps/hashes/failures; web-tool reviews supplement blocked pages. Fiscal.ai current activity was verified through its dated changelog via web; Nosto through its current product and recent launch page via web. Publication/crawl dates are not substituted for the review date. Public emails are routing contacts, not permission to market or proven delivery.
