# 30-day commercial validation experiment

DRAFT. Day 1 starts only after an approved outreach cohort and messaging are recorded, not on the date these documents were generated. No expected return, success probability or traction is asserted.

## Funnel definitions

- Relevant company: verified active product with an AI-assisted shopping, pricing, purchasing or financial-research workflow; one legal/company-level row, aliases deduplicated.
- Named decision-maker: public role verified with dated source; a role title without a person is not counted.
- Approved outreach attempt: explicit approval reference plus actual sent timestamp, channel, recipient and message hash/receipt. A draft is not an attempt.
- Reply: actual inbound reply with record; automated acknowledgments separately marked.
- Qualified conversation: two-way discussion with an authorized owner who confirms a relevant historical workflow, permissible access to cases and a concrete review need. A polite reply or demo request alone does not qualify.
- Specific scope request: buyer asks for written scope for a named workflow and exactly three eligible historical decisions, with evidence/permission constraints identified.
- Paid Fit Check/Pilot: cleared-payment evidence linked to agreed scope; invoices or verbal interest are not revenue.
- Attributable revenue: cash collected attributable to this cohort less refunds; report recognized revenue separately if accounting timing differs.

## Schedule and gates

Days 1–5: validate current named owners and public contact routes; deduplicate existing CRM/calendar history; obtain individual outreach approval. Keep blocked contacts out of send counts.
Days 6–14: send only approved messages, record actual receipts and replies. No automatic follow-ups; obtain approval for each sequence scope. Compare evidence-review language across small qualitative cohorts, not unsupported conversion statistics.
Days 15–21: conduct approved conversations; document workflow, case permissions, data minimization and constraints. Offer the existing fixed Fit Check only where it fits.
Days 22–30: reconcile scope requests, cleared payments, delivery and objections. Preserve NO FIT results and nonresponse.

Proposed commercial gate: at least TWO qualified buyer conversations and ONE specific scope request. This is a learning threshold, not proof of product-market fit. If unmet, retain the evidence and revise cohort/message/scope; do not claim market validation. Paid work is separately reported and never assumed from reaching the gate.

## Revenue treatment

One $750 Fit Check followed by an eligible $2,500 pilot uses the $750 credit: additional cash for that pilot is $1,750, total cumulative cash $2,500, not $3,250. Track the credit explicitly. A separate ineligible pilot retains published terms; never infer eligibility without the start dates and agreement. Do not count pipeline value as revenue.
