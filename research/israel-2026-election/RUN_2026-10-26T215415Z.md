## IPR: variables that could change interpretation

Threshold exposure is discontinuous: a small vote-share change around 3.25% can alter the full seat allocation. Turnout composition and nonresponse can shift measured shares; early reporting geography and later special envelopes can shift provisional returns. Surplus-vote allocation and list identity need official confirmation for final scoring. Government formation depends on subsequent political commitments, not just summed seats.

These are causal mechanisms and monitoring priorities, not claims that any particular shift will occur.
