# Conditional coalition seat forecasts — October 28 PKT

Live explorer: https://kahrelum.com/election-live#coalitions

The user requested possible coalition participants and forecast percentages. The explorer uses existing archived K-PREDICT v0.6 joint seat draws without changing model parameters or any frozen forecasts. For each selected set it reports the frequency of at least61 cabinet seats, at least61 cabinet-plus-outside-support seats, and more yes than no votes under fixed roles, full attendance, and all unselected parties opposing. Cabinet membership, outside support and abstention are explicitly different roles. These events overlap and probabilities are not expected to sum100%.

No political agreement or actual government-formation probability is estimated. Seat availability is not party willingness, legal appointment or government survival. Whole-party scenarios do not model individual defections. Six arithmetic/role tests pass; browser tests reproduce84.8% outside-support arithmetic and17.3% conditional abstention-confidence arithmetic for the captured Student nowcast, while cabinet61+ remains0.7% in those examples. Those figures are conditional on the captured prediction, not permanent or calibrated political-outcome probabilities.

Eight presets include each base bloc; Eisenkot with Raam in cabinet; outside Arab-list support or abstention; Eisenkot with Shas and UTJ; Netanyahu with Reservists–Economic; and Likud/Yashar/Together. Dated source notes flag Eisenkot’s reported Raam conditions and Netanyahu-leadership rejection, Liberman’s minority-government rejection, opposition cooperation, and unconfirmed Haredi flexibility. No source is treated as proof of a post-election joining agreement. Position notes are reviewed at this capture, not represented as an unattended real-time negotiations feed.

Current and frozen-input model views and all three existing uncertainty methods are available. The public draw JSON is numerical research output only. It allows reproducible browser-side role arithmetic; original proprietary source implementation is not published. Normalized scenario snapshots, sources, issue times and draw identities are preserved in the accompanying record. The collector updates coalition output when its prediction references change, and the page fetches updates everyminute.

A count-progress panel now shows counted ballots, completion/remaining percentages, and remaining ballots only if an official ballot denominator is explicitly verified and compatible. Missing fields remain Unknown. Exit polls, turnout and polling-station coverage cannot populate these fields. Verified official counting and final scoring remain unavailable; the coalition model is still exit-informed research.

Original C0 and scoring protocol remain unchanged. Neither the dashboard nor these conditional scenario calculations constitute witnessed immutable v2.3.4 runtime execution, calibrated government-formation forecasting or established superiority.
