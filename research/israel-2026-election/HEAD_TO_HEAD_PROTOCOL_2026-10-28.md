# KAHRELUM versus Israel Votes — post-close comparison lock

The owner requested a live head-to-head comparison and a visible separation of Netanyahu and Gadi Eisenkot blocs. Public dashboard: https://kahrelum.com/election-live#competition

The supplied domain israelelections.org was inaccessible and produced no exact search match. The explicitly disclosed comparator is Israel Votes at https://www.israelielection.org, already identified in original C0. Its deployed read-only /api/live-forecast endpoint is used for current estimates; its repository live-forecast.json is an older prior and is not substituted for deployed live output. The saved campaign forecast is retained separately as context.

The first captured pair and score plan are frozen in the accompanying JSON. Primary score: mean absolute error of each model's marginal median seats over the identical 14-list universe, lower wins and exact equality is a tie. Secondary diagnostics retain L1, RMSE, coherent 120-seat allocation L1, strict-largest multiclass Brier over14 lists plus ties, and80% interval coverage. Each model's original estimator and interval mass remain explicit. Marginal medians may not sum120 and are never rescaled. KAHRELUM's80% quantiles and medians are computed from its already archived joint draws; no model retuning or new source input is introduced for the comparison.

The original KAHRELUM90% interval display remains alongside the main source comparison. The competitive table uses80% ranges for both systems. Threshold probabilities are not compared: KAHRELUM seat-space representation is not the same event as the rival vote-threshold probability. Sources, issue times and panels differ; this is not an equal-information controlled experiment. Both comparison forecasts are post-close/exit-informed. A campaign forecast versus a later nowcast is not presented as a clean prospective forecasting victory. The lock was registered after election-day information was observed, without changing C0 or its scoring protocol.

Only independently verified official final seats can resolve the contest. Neither exit polls, this rival's estimates nor partial-count projections are gold. The scoring implementation rejects incomplete/unmapped labels, totals other than120, noninteger gold and incoherent probability vectors. If a previously unmodeled list gains seats, scoring is blocked pending an explicit disclosed protocol amendment. No unattended interpretation of the rival's results as official gold is allowed. A local verified-final admission record must include authoritative official URL, independent crosscheck URL, verification status and timestamp. No such gold record exists now; the live scoreboard remains NOT SCORED / NO WINNER DECLARED.

Netanyahu bloc is the fixed five-party group: Likud, Otzma Yehudit, Shas, UTJ, Religious Zionism–Zehut. Gadi Eisenkot bloc is the four-party Zionist opposition group: Yashar, Together, Democrats, Yisrael Beiteinu. Joint List and Ra'am are separate, as are the other modeled parties; those displays do not assert future coalition agreements. Eight targeted scoring tests pass, including exact-score arithmetic, ties, missing labels, invalid totals and probability normalization. No claim of superiority or calibration is made before resolution; one election cannot establish general superiority.
