# K-PREDICT v0.6 technical methodology

This release has two distinct modeling tracks. No 2026 outcome forecast is issued.

## 1. Raw-vote uncertainty capability

The raw-vote engine requires explicit, complete, mutually exclusive party vote proportions, a sample size, pollster family, a cutoff, and publication custody. It refuses seat projections as vote observations and rejects missing shares. A synthetic/empirical flag prevents mixing generated examples with observations. The implementation imports the unchanged v0.5 Bader–Ofer component and transforms each simulated vote composition into exactly 120 seats, using the supplied election threshold and surplus agreements. An unresolved allocation draw aborts the run rather than being silently discarded.

Only the newest eligible observation per research-house family contributes. Recency weights use a fixed seven-day half-life. Effective sampling size conservatively equals the smallest included house sample, rather than summing correlated samples. A Jeffreys-style 0.5 count per party permits an observed zero without treating it as certain impossibility. One method uses a Dirichlet sampling distribution plus normal systematic logratio error. The API method `logistic_normal` is precisely a logistic-normal scale mixture: independent normal logratio noise is multiplied by a common variance-normalized Student-t(5) scale. Softmax closure induces negative dependence and the shared scale adds common tail dependence. Sampling logratio variance is 1/(n*p), whose first-order closed-composition covariance is (diag(p)-p*p')/n. The systematic logratio standard deviation 0.08 is an explicit assumed input, not learned from seat-only historical records.

Turnout multipliers are positive party-specific scenario weights applied before normalization. They are sensitivity assumptions, not empirically estimated turnout forecasts. Integer vote totals of 100,000,000 provide numerical precision only; they are not an effective sample size or electorate prediction.

The included synthetic demonstration compares both distributions. It is software capability evidence only. Historical execution remains blocked if no full raw-vote vector and appropriate provenance are available. No latent or observed vote percentages are inferred by dividing polling seats by 120.

## 2. Historical seat-space diagnostic

Historical observations remain seat projections. The initial diagnostic data consist of the inherited 21 retrospectively chosen snapshots across seven elections, with D−14, D−7 and D−2 cutoffs. The prediction party universe comes from explicitly enumerated, available poll vectors, not final results. It excludes never-polled minor lists and therefore cannot estimate full-ballot representation performance. Missing labels are not silently filled with zeros. The original 2013 D−14 two derived zero cells remain labeled by the inherited record; excluding all2013 training data is a conservative sensitivity.

Cutoffs are end of day,23:59:59 Asia/Jerusalem, on election date minus horizon days. Publication dates in inherited rows are archive table dates, not verified first-publication timestamps. A primary page updated after a cutoff is excluded for that cutoff. Publication, fieldwork and later page-update dates are different fields. All historical results are exploratory and provisional, because no immutable historical source snapshot or untouched model-selection experiment has been established.

Six fixed candidates are retained: latest published seat map; house-balanced average; recency-weighted house average; fixed Gaussian seat-error model; regularized Gaussian error; and correlated Student-t(5) error. An average uses only the latest observation per named house group. Inherited house grouping is a heuristic based on supplied labels; it is not an estimate of cross-house independence. These are averages of a sparse, selected archive, not a claim to exhaustively reconstruct every contemporary poll.

For each target election and horizon, the fitted error variance uses only strictly earlier elections at the same horizon. Each earlier election contributes its mean squared party error of its recency-weighted center, regardless of party count. The variance estimate is (4*9 + sum earlier election MSE)/(4 + earlier election count), with floor1 seat-squared. Thus four prior-election equivalents shrink toward a fixed standard deviation3 seats. No full party-by-party covariance or house offset is fitted: seven distinct elections cannot identify them adequately.

Gaussian errors are centered to sum zero and standardized to marginal variance1. The Student candidate uses the same errors multiplied by sqrt((5−2)/ChiSquare5), creating common tail dependence. Simulated continuous seat values undergo an explicit hurdle heuristic. Lists with noisy value at least m−0.5 are active, where m=2 in2013 and3 later. Each active list receives m reserved seats, then remaining seats are assigned by Hamilton rounding of positive excess values. All joint draws sum120. This is NOT a vote-threshold calculation or Bader–Ofer simulation. It is a constrained seat-space probability model, and its representation probabilities must not be described as true vote-share threshold probabilities.

The deterministic measurement baselines retain rounded observed seat maps even where an old published map has a subminimum count. Their legal coherence is not certified. The statistical model has different support by design; this restriction itself is an assumption requiring sensitivity review.

## Evaluation and scoring

2013 is prior-only warmup.2015,April2019,September2019,2020,2021 and2022 form six later election clusters. Three horizons per election are repeated contexts, not eighteen independent elections. Parameters fit only earlier outcomes, but model design and archive selection happened after all seven outcomes were known; no final case is untouched.

For each named party/context: representation is seats>0; binary Brier is(p−y)^2; seat multiclass Brier is the sum over121 seat counts of(p_s−1[s=y])^2; normalized ranked probability score sums squared CDF error at cutpoints0..119 and divides120. Posterior-mean absolute seat error, squared error, total L1 seat error and per-context RMSE are reported.90% intervals are discrete equal-tail5th/95th percentiles. Representation classification uses p>=0.5. This last rate is not a vote-threshold error rate.

Main scores use raw empirical PMFs from coherent joint simulations. Zero predicted probability of an observed outcome yields infinite raw logloss, represented as null plus an explicit infinity flag and count. A separately labeled floor1e−6 logloss diagnostic is finite; it is not the unmodified proper log score. No marginal smoothing changes the Brier/RPS/interval probabilities. More draws improve numerical precision, not evidence of empirical accuracy.

Primary aggregate scores average contexts within each election, then average the six elections. Party-weighted values are secondary, with explicit party-context denominator. `RMSE_seats` in the election-balanced table is mean fold RMSE; the party-weighted table includes pooled RMSE. Baselines include latest poll, p=.5 and a Beta(1,1)-regularized historical representation rate averaging earlier election-level rates. Beating p=.5 is not evidence of superiority over the published poll baseline.

Generic parliamentary aggregates are represented-list count and strictly largest party versus TIE. No after-the-fact political blocs are created. RPS is not applied to unordered party labels. Differences versus latest poll use paired election-cluster bootstrap with20,000 draws and leave-one-election-out sensitivity. Six clusters give limited inferential power, and bootstrap intervals do not include archive-selection or source-vintage uncertainty.

## Calibration and model selection

Reliability bins use fixed0.1 probability widths. They report party-context counts and contributing election clusters without naive independent-binomial intervals. The separate marginal recalibration diagnostic uses fixed0.2 bins and four prior-election equivalents. Only earlier elections' predictions/outcomes contribute, each election with total weight1 across all its horizons/parties. For a current probability p in bin b, p_cal=(4*p+weighted previous events in b)/(4+previous weight in b). These recalibrated marginals are NOT substituted into the joint seat distribution. Every candidate and recalibration result is retained; none is selected as a validated winner.

## Failure and review trail

The first attempted aggregate run failed on a finite/null raw-logloss aggregation bug; its original log is retained. The first test-discovery invocation found zero tests because of an output-path error; that is retained as failure, not counted as passing. The first-order raw-vote sampling covariance was corrected following an independent reviewer calculation before first successful execution, with the original code hash preserved by the reviewer. First successful outputs and source hashes are captured separately in LEAD_INITIAL_EVALUATION.json and initial_outputs/ before later changes.

## Final expanded data and conservative admission

The final normalized dataset contains41 records across the same seven elections. The runner examines expanded primary records but admits only complete numeric120-seat vectors with exactly the pre-cutoff inherited polling roster. It excludes aggregates, missing cells, mismatched rosters, later-updated pages and duplicate underlying polls. A fuller inherited secondary vector may remain as a provisional fallback when the corresponding newly retrieved primary extraction lacks peripheral-party cells; both are never counted together. This choice does not promote the secondary observation to primary custody.

The two inferred2013 D−14 zero cells and the inherited2020 D−2 dash were restored to null in normalized data; the original21-row file remains preserved separately. Consequently2013 D−14 has no eligible complete vector and is explicitly BLOCKED in final outputs, rather than silently imputed or removed from the requirements ledger. Earlier complete polls and a verified complete primary projection permit the2020 D−2 diagnostic. Final execution has20 available election/horizon contexts: two2013 warmup contexts and18 later held-out contexts,6 later election clusters and213 party/horizon events per candidate. None of the41 records has strict authenticated pre-cutoff custody.

The raw-vote API now requires an independently verified as-of roster and complete as-of surplus-agreement evidence for empirical execution, validates fieldwork against publication, rejects a later-updated source without a verified earlier immutable capture, and refuses conflicting same-house/same-timestamp observations. Structural guards also reject unmatched draw columns, noninteger/invalid seats, duplicate parties, invalid gold vectors, invalid rates and unknown candidate names. These gates validate the supplied evidence contract; they do not independently authenticate caller-supplied evidence flags.

Twelve synthetic scenarios execute both uncertainty methods at thresholds2% and3.25%, crossed with a near-threshold minor-list turnout multiplier0.85,1.0 and1.15, using common random seeds. All are labeled synthetic and none is historical calibration evidence. The complete current test suite includes51 new tests, separate from the inherited118 engineering tests and independent reviewers' checks.
