The newspaper reproduces language it attributes to the instruction calling for a probe to substantiate or refute suspicions. Neither country is established as a funder; financial channels, transfers, source identity, causal influence and electoral effect remain unverified. Attribution to the news report is NOT direct first-party authentication of the order.

**What did not happen:** No court judgment invalidated ballots, no nationwide fraud established, no legal postponement, no election suspension and no government-formation determination. Do not equate an investigative instruction with confirmed foreign interference. Reported cash envelopes are allegations until independently substantiated.
