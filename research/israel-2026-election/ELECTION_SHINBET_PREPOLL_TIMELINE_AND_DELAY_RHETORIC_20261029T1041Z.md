## FACT / REPORTED: what changed in source knowledge

1. **Shin Bet / election chairman reporting chronology:** The Times of Israel **11:44 IST** liveblog entry, citing Ynet, Channel 12 and Kan, reports that Shin Bet chief David Zini requested an October 28 private meeting with Central Elections Committee (CEC) chair Justice Noam Sohlberg to raise suspicions of foreign election interference. An unnamed security source quoted by Kan claims an investigation had already begun **before election day**, within the preceding two weeks. Independently, Ynet's Oct 29 report states Shin Bet already had information for days before Sohlberg's written instruction. **This is attributed reporting, not a publicly authenticated Shin Bet investigative-start record, and is not confirmation that foreign interference actually occurred.**

Ynet quotes him declining to attest to the allegations' quality. The letter is an investigative referral, **not a finding of bribery, a certification of fraud, or a legal rescheduling**. The original signed instruction was not independently acquired by this monitor; quotes from press must retain secondary-source provenance.

Yashar leader Gadi Eisenkot warned against exploiting the inquiry to influence the next Knesset's swearing-in date, as reported by The Times of Israel at **10:31 IST**. These are statements, **not a filed legal motion, court order, election commission suspension, or effective change of date**. Likud's previous vote-theft claims and earlier reported police rebuttal must remain in the ledger, not superseded.

This is a newspaper's description of applicable law, not an independently retrieved statute, filed appeal or judgment. No actual election-result annulment or statutory postponement was verified in the present run.
