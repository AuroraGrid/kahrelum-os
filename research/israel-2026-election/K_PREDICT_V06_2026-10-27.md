## What was executed

The owner package contains reproducible probabilistic research code for Israeli proportional-representation experiments; normalized historical polling evidence; chronological, retrospective seat-model evaluations; calibration diagnostics; same-target baselines; independent AI-agent reviews; and numerical integrity controls. It covers exactly January 22 2013, March 17 2015, April 9 2019, September 17 2019, March 2 2020, March 23 2021 and November 1 2022. No earlier election is included.

Two tracks are deliberately separate:

1. **Genuine vote-share uncertainty capability:** a logistic-normal Student-t scale-mixture specification and a Dirichlet specification accept complete, genuine vote-share vectors and sample sizes, model sampling/systematic uncertainty, then apply the unchanged Bader–Ofer allocator to simulated integer votes. Inputs, roster, dates and surplus agreements are subject to explicit evidence gates. Synthetic demonstrations exercise threshold and turnout behavior. They are not historical validation.
2. **Historical seat-space evaluation:** the observations are published seat projections. A sum-constrained seat-error model produces joint 120-seat distributions and representation probabilities. Its hurdle and Hamilton redistribution are explicitly a heuristic; they do not execute Bader–Ofer on historical uncertain votes and cannot validate genuine vote-threshold probabilities.

No complete usable historical raw vote-share polling vector with an independently verified denominator was recovered. Nine true 2022 percentage entries totaling 86.6% were retained as partial evidence without normalization. Seat projections were never relabeled as observed vote shares. This missing data prevents the central historical validation of the genuine vote-share track.

### Exact score definitions

- Representation Brier: mean of(p−1[official seats>0])²; denominator is the declared party-horizon population, with election-balanced and pooled summaries distinguished.
- Seat multiclass Brier: sum of 121 squared category-probability errors for counts0–120; range0–2.
- Seat RPS: sum of 120 cumulative squared errors at cutpoints0–119, divided by 120.
- Raw log loss uses the actual empirical PMF; zero realized mass is infinite, represented with an explicit flag/null numeric field. Separately labeled1e−6-clipped diagnostics are not a silent replacement.
- Seat MAE/L1/RMSE concern the PMF mean point prediction. Mean-context RMSE differs from pooled RMSE; both are labeled in machine outputs.
- 90% intervals are central quantiles of each model's seat distribution. Point-poll baselines are imposed point masses, not confidence intervals published by pollsters.
- Representation classification error is not a direct vote-threshold forecast error.
- Aggregate distributions cover the number of represented lists and the strict largest list, including ties. Multiclass scores apply to unordered largest-list labels; RPS is not applied to arbitrary party-label order.
