## Executive conclusion

A new, stdlib-only Python election-analytics implementation was executed outside the immutable KAHRELUM OS v2.3.4 runtime. It reconstructs Israeli vote-to-seat arithmetic from known vote totals, including the 3.25% valid-ballot threshold, Bader–Ofer remainder allocation and documented bilateral surplus-vote agreements; it also tests published 2026 survey-vector coherence and disagreement. Its source code is preserved in an owner-controlled package rather than exposed in this public report.

**This is not a proprietary KAHRELUM prediction of the October 27 outcome.** No first-party 2026 party-seat vector, winner probability, threshold-pass probability, or Brier score was computed or frozen. Tests of historical vote counting do not establish an ability to predict voter behavior. No proper claim of forecast skill is made.
