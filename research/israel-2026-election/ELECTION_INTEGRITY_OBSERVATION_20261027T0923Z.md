## 1. New police enforcement action: reported factual arrest, alleged attempted bribery

**FACT (police announcement as separately reproduced by named media):** Israeli police announced that they arrested three residents of Segev Shalom and Ar'ara BaNegev overnight before October 27 polling on suspicion of an election-bribery scheme. According to police, searches yielded envelopes with tens of thousands of shekels, ballot slips and additional suspected evidence. The suspects were being questioned and were expected to face a remand hearing.

**ALLEGATION UNDER INVESTIGATION:** Police believe the money was intended to induce voters to support Ra'am. This has NOT been adjudicated as proven bribery, is NOT a verified tally of actual purchased votes, and does NOT establish involvement by Ra'am officials or leadership.
