## The buyer problem
An AI system can assemble evidence for an audit, compliance determination, claim or payment, but a polished packet does not prove every necessary fact was present or every decision had the required authorization at the cutoff. The independent question is: **would the record still justify the historical decision after a skeptical, source-bound review?**

KAHRELUM offers a bounded historical AI-decision evidence review. We do not conduct licensed financial statement audits, regulatory certifications or penetration tests. No DoW client, contractor or access is claimed.

## Public-record example: separate three claims
This example checks statements about the **Pentagon's public FY2026 audit announcement**. It demonstrates claim discipline. It is **not** an execution of a KAHRELUM customer Fit Check, since public statements are not three permissioned historical AI-agent decisions.

### Claim 1: "56% of assets and over 90% of liabilities have clean opinions."
**Classification:** FORECAST-INCLUSIVE / MISLEADING WITHOUT TIME QUALIFIER.  
**Evidence:** The department's Nov. 16 release explicitly includes component opinions expected in early 2027. Reuters reported a 56% / 86% coverage snapshot, while another source described 12 signed opinions plus four projected, with different likely cutoffs or definitions.  
**Provisional action:** HOLD any unqualified assertion that >90% has already been independently certified.  
**Unlock:** Dates, entity definitions and signed component opinions aligned.

### Claim 2: "The Pentagon has a $200 million AI audit allocation."
**Classification:** SUPPORTED as a **funding plan**, not as proof of spending.  
**Evidence:** The May 4 mandatory funding allocation plan assigns $200m across five audit-AI categories, including $30m for gathering evidential matter.  
**Provisional action:** PASS only a wording that says *allocated/planned*.  
**Unlock for vendor revenue claims:** obligation records, award IDs, scope, competition and contracting eligibility.

### Claim 3: "AI has solved the audit-evidence weaknesses."
**Classification:** NOT PROVEN.  
**Evidence:** GAO cautioned that the revised approach may defer underlying internal control remediation; FY2026 agency-wide audit was still reported as disclaimed. The War Data Platform's specific SOC 1 Type 2 outcome is positive but not a global validation of AI outputs.  
**Provisional action:** HOLD.  
**Unlock:** independent output-level validation against ground truth, measurable error/exception rates, and signed opinion findings.

## Example buyer-side acceptance protocol
For one client's **authorized, de-identified, historical workflow**, request exactly three closed AI-assisted decisions, each with a decision cutoff, relevant contemporaneous source snapshots, action taken, governing rules, approvals and escalation logs. Agree what FIT/NO-FIT means before viewing them.

Check the following in every case: source provenance; completeness; evidence available at cutoff; contradictory evidence; permissions; human approval/escalation; falsifying evidence; uncertainty; repeatability. Report gaps even when the result is NO-FIT.

**No data or source?** Mark NOT ASSESSABLE rather than inventing it. No client data in public GitHub or website inquiry. Buyers retain control over redaction and permitted scope. Independence or conflicts are reviewed before engagement.
