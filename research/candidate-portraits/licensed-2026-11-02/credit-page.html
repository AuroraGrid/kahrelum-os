How this was built

MT26 is a live forecast of the 2026 US midterm elections: all 435 House seats, the 35 Senate races and the 36 governor races, 506 in all. One developer built it with Claude Code, Anthropic's AI coding tool. The developer set the goals and made the calls. Claude Code wrote the model, the data pipeline, the Lab and these pages.

What the model does. Each race blends up to three signals: the district's partisan lean, expert ratings and public polls. The model then simulates the election 20,000 times, with errors shared across similar races, so a polling miss in one state tends to show up in its neighbours too. "88 of 100" means that party won control in 88 of every 100 simulated elections. It is a forecast, not a result, and a 1-in-5 outcome happens one time in five.

How it runs. A Cloudflare Worker serves the site. Polls and ratings refresh every hour until Election Day morning, and the forecast is re-run after each refresh. The Lab has four interactive views, two of them in 3D, drawn from the same simulations. Full details and CSV downloads are on the methodology page.

What it is not. MT26 is independent and non-commercial, and not affiliated with any campaign, party or outlet. Visits are counted in two ways, and neither uses cookies or local storage. The site's own counter stores no IP addresses: for each page load it records only the page, campaign tags, referring site, country, device type, a bot yes/no, time on page and which views were opened, nothing that identifies a visitor, and it is read back in aggregate. For visitors outside the EU, Cloudflare Web Analytics also counts page views and loading speed, without following anyone from visit to visit by IP address or browser.

Tips. MT26 is free, with no ads. If it helped you, you can leave a tip on Ko-fi. Tips go to the developer, not to any campaign, party or cause.
