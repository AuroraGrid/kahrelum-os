The purpose is not to present speculation as reporting. Each dossier separates:

- **FACT** — directly supported by a cited public source.
- **INFERENCE** — a reasoned interpretation that goes beyond the source.
- **OPEN QUESTION** — an issue the available evidence does not resolve.

## Research standard

KAHRELUM uses a source hierarchy that prefers primary legal, regulatory, company, and technical records before relying on secondary reporting. Secondary reporting is used when it contains material not available in primary records, and is labeled accordingly.

Claims are kept narrow. Correlation is not treated as causation. Missing evidence is recorded rather than silently filled. Adverse or exculpatory evidence is preserved.
