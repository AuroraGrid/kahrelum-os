# Anthropic’s lobbying, Rubio connections and the evidence gap around Jensen Huang

**REVIEW DRAFT — HOLD FOR SOURCE VERIFICATION. Checked November 17, 2026. Not published.**

Federal records establish paid representation and professional connections. They do not establish that Anthropic orchestrated Jensen Huang’s departure from Nvidia.

## What the money establishes

The [official federal lobbying register](https://lda.gov/filings/public/filing/search/?client=anthropic&search=search) lists the following income reported by Continental Strategy and Ballard Partners for Anthropic. KAHRELUM independently recalculated the totals from that register. These are lobbying-income estimates, not audited bank payments, personal payments to officials or evidence of a payment for a particular government decision.

|---|---:|---:|
| 2025 Q2 | $90,000 | — |
| 2025 Q3 | $90,000 | — |
| 2025 Q4 | $110,000 | — |
| 2026 Q1 | $150,000 | $130,000 |
| 2026 Q2 | $150,000 | $380,000 |
| 2026 Q3 | $150,000 | $380,000 |
| Selected-series total | **$740,000** | **$890,000** |

The combined figure is **$1.63 million** across different reporting periods. A dash means no amount is included for Ballard in this series, not that Anthropic had no other lobbying expenses. These two firms are not Anthropic’s entire lobbying operation. Adding outside-firm income to Anthropic’s separately reported expenses risks counting overlapping spending twice.

The inspected Continental Q1 2026 form describes its income as a good-faith estimate rounded to the nearest $10,000. The official index supports all nine amounts; full-body retrieval of eight quarterly reports failed on follow-up. Their issue descriptions, personnel changes and amendments remain to be reconciled before release.

## A real Rubio connection, with a specific evidentiary limit

[Continental’s registration](https://lda.gov/filings/public/filing/bcba8bc2-f110-44eb-b076-b49bedea9a7c/print/) was effective **April 24, 2025**, and posted **May 7, 2025**. It identifies Alberto Martinez’s prior position as chief of staff to Senator Marco Rubio. It lists Martinez and Paul Calkins Jr. and describes an AI-development and national-security assignment.

The [Q1 2026 Continental report](https://lda.gov/filings/public/filing/7f7435b4-526f-4004-99d2-83d9becd5186/print/) reports $150,000 and lists Martinez, Paul Calkins and Carlos Trujillo. It identifies Congress and the Executive Office of the President as contacted institutions. It does not identify a conversation with Rubio about Huang, a client instruction to seek Huang’s departure, or any quid pro quo.

This distinction matters: a former government role establishes a professional connection. It does not establish that the former official obtained a particular outcome from that office.

## Ballard’s declared assignment was procurement

[Ballard’s registration](https://lda.gov/filings/public/filing/bed8a620-9864-40dc-b2f7-ad2983a33d9b/print/) was effective **March 9, 2026**, but signed and posted **April 10, 2026**. Filing date and engagement-effective date must not be conflated.

The form names Brian Ballard, Sylvester Lukis and Micah Ketchel. Ketchel disclosed State Department and White House advisory positions in 2025 and prior service as Mike Waltz’s congressional chief of staff. The stated assignment is **“Advocacy regarding DOW procurement.”** The registration does not describe a campaign against Huang or justify attributing Ballard’s entire fee to chip-export lobbying.

Claims about additional personnel and family connections—including Katie Wiles, Susie Wiles and Amaryllis Kennedy—remain outside the verified findings pending primary-record checks. Employment at a firm would not itself establish work on Anthropic’s account. No family relationship is evidence of wrongdoing.

## The chip-policy dispute needs its own evidence

Anthropic’s [April 29, 2025 diffusion-rule submission](https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/7449887b6715e3a35f362b1301e5b5d8a6b116e5.pdf) is a relevant primary-source lead. Indexed text supports its advocacy for stronger compute controls and support for restrictions on Nvidia’s H20. Full PDF retrieval returned HTTP 403. The complete argument and Nvidia’s response must be inspected before release rather than reconstructed from excerpts.

An independently downloaded [Federal Register rule, document 2026-00789](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-01-15/pdf/2026-00789.pdf), establishes a separate policy milestone. Effective **January 15, 2026**, BIS changed the review policy for qualifying H200 and equivalent exports to China and Macau from a presumption of denial to case-by-case consideration, subject to conditions. This was not an unrestricted export authorization. The January 13 announcement and January 15 effective date are distinct. The historical rule alone does not establish the entire policy in force in November.

Security arguments and commercial incentives can coexist. An AI developer may favor tighter controls while having business interests affected by them; a chip seller may favor overseas market access while arguing that American technology leadership benefits security. Those possibilities do not identify the cause of an executive’s departure.

The [Commerce–Justice statement on Megaspeed](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/joint-statement-department-commerce-and-department-justice-megaspeed-investigation) places the investigation’s origin before both registrations. That weighs against the claim that either engagement initiated this particular investigation. It does not resolve every possible later-influence claim. The statement establishes the government’s account, not criminal guilt.

“Resignation,” removal from a travel delegation and board dismissal are different events. This draft does not treat them as interchangeable. Relevant reporting leads are [Reuters, syndicated November 12](https://ca.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resigns-in-blow-to-ai-boom-4953725), and [NDTV Profit’s November 14 memo account](https://www.ndtvprofit.com/business/was-it-really-the-affair-the-secret-memo-that-complicates-the-story-of-jensen-huangs-nvidia-exit-12325296). Search excerpts were available, but complete reporting and issuer reconciliation were not completed. The memo’s contents and causal significance are not admitted proof.
