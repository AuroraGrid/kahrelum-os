Frequently asked questions before requesting a review

What exactly is in the $750 Fit Check?

One agreed workflow and exactly three authorized, de-identified closed historical decisions. The deliverable identifies support gaps, limits, and a FIT / NO FIT finding. A NO FIT outcome is a valid final result. The target is two business days after written scope, permitted inputs, and cleared payment.

What changes in the $2,500 Founding Pilot?

One workflow and up to ten permissioned historical decisions, with individual evidence records, a consolidated report, and one readout. The target is five business days after scope and protocol freeze, cleared payment and usable approved inputs. The $750 Fit Check is credited if the pilot begins within 14 days.

Do you need production access or customer records?

No production access is required or offered by these fixed-scope reviews. Do not submit customer identifiers, restricted prompts, live credentials or private records through the website. Scope and permitted de-identified inputs must be agreed separately before any work begins.

Does the website automatically send a request or take payment?

No. The public inquiry prepares an email locally for you to review and send manually; it is not automatically sent or stored by the form. There is no immediate website checkout. Written scope, supplier eligibility, permission and payment instructions are agreed before work starts.
