KAHRELUM ONE / Product development

One assistant for life, not just one kind of question.

KAHRELUM ONE is the planned consumer face of the evidence-to-decision system. Talk naturally about anything: practical work, life, learning, sports, creativity or difficult decisions. Advanced verification should be available when helpful, not make ordinary chat feel like filing paperwork.

Register early interestSee the underlying approach

Product principles

Everyday first. Evidence when it matters.

Broad by default

General-purpose support, not limited to politics, investigations, business or technical tasks.

Easy from the first message

A clear chat experience for friends and everyday users, with optional guided modes rather than dense controls.

Bring receipts to important claims

When uncertainty matters, source verification, competing explanations and explicit unknowns help users judge for themselves.

Current status: In development. No publicly available Android release, working invite-code gate, paid subscription, autonomous agent, live backend or production functionality is represented by this page. This is a concept overview only.

Separate consumer product, same underlying values

KAHRELUM ONE is distinct from paid enterprise assurance and public investigations. It is the interface for everyday questions, while the OS provides structured checks when necessary. Availability, pricing and platform support will be announced only after implementation and testing.
