Meta multistate consent judgment: signed is not paid

Source review: 2026-11-01 · Historical event dates appear in the evidence record

Original finding
The August 26 multistate announcement described a settlement still subject to approval at announcement time.
Updated evidence

AIR-01Portfolio: OPEN

Learjet 60 N722BD Crash, Palm Beach County, Florida

Aviation / Public Safety / Security Hypothesis

ACTIVE / CAUSE UNDETERMINED · Incident: November 1, 2026

Original finding
New active investigation, separate from the historical 56+4 inventory. FACT: the FAA reports a Learjet 60 crash near Cresthaven around 12:50 p.m. local on November 1, with nine people aboard. In Florida this is EST (17:50 UTC); the FAA time is approximate.
Updated evidence
FACT: the FAA identifies departure from Austin-Bergstrom and destination North Eleuthera, Bahamas, and says NTSB leads the investigation. The FAA registry identifies N722BD as Learjet INC model 60, serial 271, registered to LR60271 LLC. The registry does not prove incident-flight operational control or airworthiness. ALLEGATION / operator lead: an August 12, 2025 commercial listing associates N722BD with Aurora Aviation; that relationship has not been authenticated from the operator or FAA operating specifications for this flight. Local 10 attributes nine fatalities to the transportation secretary and reproduces an NTSB go-team announcement; the original announcements remain to be captured.
Decision significance
No authenticated evidence establishes a Trump or Mar-a-Lago connection. Whether this was an intentional message or threat is UNKNOWN. Geographical proximity and reported presidential movements do not establish intent. No official security finding or independently authenticated White House / Secret Service chronology was recovered. No numerical probability or FORECAST is made.

What remains unknown: Exact departure time, cleared route, altitude history and complete raw ADS-B track; authenticated ATC audio and transcript; incident-specific weather, icing and turbulence records; passenger and crew identities; mechanical condition, maintenance, recorder contents and official wreckage examination. AP describes icing, turbulence and a rapid descent from reported ATC and tracking data; these remain secondary-source leads pending primary-record authentication. No case-specific CAROL number, preliminary findings or final cause report has been authenticated. Missing records are not evidence that none exist.

Primary sources, unresolved records and falsifiers

Competing hypotheses — analytical INFERENCE, not findings:

  • Weather-related loss of control: strengthen with time-and-position matched radar, pilot reports, icing data and recorders; weaken if the authenticated track and recordings show benign conditions or another initiating event.
  • Mechanical failure: strengthen with physical fracture, system, maintenance or recorder evidence; weaken with documented component integrity and a demonstrated alternative cause.
  • Crew or operational factors: strengthen with authenticated clearances, training, loading, checklists and recorder evidence; weaken if compliance is established and an external initiating cause is demonstrated.
  • Sabotage: requires forensic tampering, explosives, access or credible communications evidence. No direct evidence authenticated; official forensic exclusion or a demonstrated accidental causal chain would weaken it.
  • Political-security scenario: requires authenticated targeting, intent, communications or an official investigative link. No such evidence authenticated. An unrelated flight purpose and an independently established accidental chain would weaken it; location alone cannot strengthen intent.

Next investigative records: NTSB case identification, preliminary report and CAROL docket; FAA incident report, operating specifications and flight plan; timestamped raw ADS-B and original ATC archive; NOAA/NWS observations and advisories; county scene reports, official identity releases and wreckage findings; independently documented presidential chronology only if relevant. Preserve originals and append corrections.

Two-camera evidence and high-altitude upset (November 2 update)
