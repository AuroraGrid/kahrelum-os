Meta multistate consent judgment: signed is not paid

Source review: 2026-11-01 · Historical event dates appear in the evidence record

Original finding
The August 26 multistate announcement described a settlement still subject to approval at announcement time.
Updated evidence
A Kentucky Attorney General legislative briefing documents a federal consent judgment entered August 26, 2026 (MDL 3047, Dkt. 3451), with appeal rights waived for that action. Meta's October 21 SEC exhibit reports a $9.89 billion Q3 accounting charge and up to $18.59 billion for a broader agreement involving 52 attorneys general. The August state announcement described up to $17.1 billion. Different scopes, later additions and accounting categories must not be added together. The settled claims were contested; a consent judgment is not an admission of liability.
Decision significance
Correct the original approval-status shorthand. Keep federal judgment, related state actions, accrued expense, cash collection and implementation as distinct events.
Primary sources, unresolved records and falsifiers

Needed to close or correct: Signed judgment and amendments; updated allocation table; each jurisdiction's receipts; compliance audit; docket modifications or enforcement orders. Payment and performance remain unknown.

October FOMC hold: event resolved, independent custody still separate

Source review: 2026-10-31 · Historical event dates appear in the evidence record

Original finding
A pre-decision KAHRELUM conversation recorded a 77% hold forecast. The October 30 register had not reconciled the official outcome; third-party custody of the exact forecast lock remained unverified.
Updated evidence
The Federal Reserve's October 28, 2026, 2 p.m. EDT statement says the FOMC voted 10–2 to maintain a 3.75%–4.00% target range. The direction is correct. A conditional binary Brier score for p=0.77 and outcome YES is 0.0529, not an independently certified KAHRELUM accuracy record.

Mark the defined rate-hold outcome observed without recasting a recovered conversational forecast as third-party authenticated performance.

DoorDash NYC restitution: $131.5 million settlement versus actual checks

Source review: 2026-10-31 · Historical event dates appear in the evidence record

Original finding
NYC announced a $131.5 million delivery-worker settlement September 22, including over $115 million intended for workers and over $16 million in penalties and costs.
Updated evidence
NYC's October 29 worker notice says affected workers were emailed a payment amount and could select a delivery method, with some checks and later-period payments planned for early 2027. Decision significance
Separate a settlement obligation, claims administration, payment-method selection and independently documented disbursement.
Primary sources, unresolved records and falsifiers

Needed to close or correct: Settlement-administrator accounting, receipts and reconciliation, compliance-monitor report, all scheduled payment batches and any amended order.
