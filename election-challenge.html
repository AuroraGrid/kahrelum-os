Brier score

For a binary probability p and outcome y: (p − y)2. Lower is better. Only forecasts with the same resolved proposition qualify.

Log loss

−[y ln(p) + (1 − y) ln(1 − p)]. Extreme, wrong confidence is penalized heavily. Probability clipping rules must be fixed before scoring.

Seat-count error

Absolute error between the forecast seat statistic and the certified total, while preserving whether the source published a mean, median, map or favored-seat count.

District comparison

Score only districts with authenticated pre-cutoff probabilities and matched candidate/event definitions. A 50% toss-up is not counted as a correct directional call.

Two chamber outcomes cannot establish general calibration or universal superiority. Unlike cutoffs are descriptive unless a matched-cutoff analysis is available. Missing records, exclusions, corrections and unfavorable results remain part of the audit.
