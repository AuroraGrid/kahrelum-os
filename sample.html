Illustrative only. This is a static, synthetic example derived from the already-published KAHRELUM demo record. The entities, counts, dates, and scenario are invented. No customer engagement or live KAHRELUM execution is represented.

Brief

Question under review

Should a team commit now to an October 31 platform migration date?

Illustrative scope only. The example contains invented scenario details, not facts about a real organization or project.

Evidence

Stipulated facts in the synthetic scenario

  • Eight of ten planned service rehearsals have passed.
  • The two remaining rehearsals cover the highest-dependency services.
  • A rollback checklist exists, but no timed rollback rehearsal has been recorded.

Inference

What the evidence supports

Passing most rehearsals is encouraging. The unresolved high-dependency cases and untested rollback leave material readiness uncertainty. The evidence supports continued preparation, not a firm date commitment.

Uncertainty

What remains unresolved

  • No history shows how well rehearsal results predict production outcomes.
  • The scope, severity, and remediation time for the two remaining services are unspecified.
  • No independently verified rollback timing or recovery objective is provided.

Counterargument

The strongest alternative reading

The team could still meet the date: eight rehearsals passed, the remaining failures may be routine, and the checklist may be enough if rollback is rarely needed. The scenario does not show that the remaining work will miss the deadline.

Evaluation / decision result

HOLD DATE COMMITMENT

Commit only after both high-dependency rehearsals pass against frozen acceptance criteria and a timed rollback rehearsal meets the agreed recovery objective. Reassess if either gate fails.

Limitations

How far this result reaches

  • This result is illustrative and synthetic, not empirical.
  • It makes no claim about a real platform, team, customer, or migration.
  • A real pilot result would be bounded by its agreed scope, protocol, evidence cutoff, and evaluation conditions.

Record

Disposition preserved

Current disposition: continue preparation; do not treat the date as cleared by this example.
