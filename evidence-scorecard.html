About this resource

Clear boundaries, useful answers.

Does a PASS mean my agent is safe? No. It means this template recorded support for one control. Even eight passes cannot certify safety, legality, governance quality or error rate.

Does KAHRELUM receive my worksheet? No. This static page makes an export in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server when you fill out or download the worksheet.

How do I receive the full Excel and PDF kit? Use the email request link above, which opens your email program. It is not an automated signup or newsletter subscription. The sender must press Send, and KAHRELUM must reply with the files.

Can I send you customer records? Not via this public form or email request. Confirm written authorization, vendor eligibility and an agreed handling process first.
