Worldwide Digital Identity Watch / Investigations

Who is changing identity, access and verification?

A global, jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction research programme distinguishing voluntary digital wallets, foundational IDs, government login, biometric registries and age assurance. Our scope is worldwide; our documented findings are not yet comprehensive.

Evidence cutoff: October 30, 2026. Not a live government database or an assertion that all countries require digital IDs.

Different systems, different legal effects

National & foundational ID

Legal identity numbers, cards and enrollment obligations.

Digital wallet & login

Digital credentials, authentication, available alternatives and service-level requirements.

Biometrics & age assurance

Collection, retention, verification scope, private providers and privacy safeguards.

No blank country becomes a forced-adoption headline.

Countries without verified source material remain NOT ASSESSED. Updates and corrections are retained.
