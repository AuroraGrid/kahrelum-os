## What is and is not established

- The v1.4 static snapshot contains 100 forecast candidates with 20 each across geopolitics, AI/technology, markets/macro, sports, and commercial/business.
- Horizon allocation is 50 short (7–30 elapsed days) and 50 medium (31–90 elapsed days), with zero technical deadline violations relative to the cited GitHub commit time.
- All 100 forecasts are RES-OPEN in the snapshot. There are no verified outcomes or Brier scores for CAL-DRILL-005 at this time.
- This is a *new* GitHub-hosted prospective custody point. It does NOT rehabilitate earlier v1.0 timestamp claims. The original 60 unattested and 40 contradictory nominal timestamps remain unverified.
- Source URLs and proposed resolution rules are visible, but independent evidence and adversarial review are incomplete. Some sources share a publisher or have encountered retrieval errors. Evidence URL count is not proof of independent corroboration.
- Seven v1.4 predictions were reissued as new propositions; earlier versions remain separate and inspectable.
- The previous seven-live-forecast October–November Brier mean (0.1444) is a distinct cohort with its own limitations and must never be attributed to this 100-forecast challenge.
- The data represent research probability judgments, not investment advice, a live trading product, or accredited forecasting certification.

## How to read a record

Use `records[].id` as the immutable versioned forecast ID. `pYes` and `pNo` are decimal probabilities summing to one. The deadline is normalized to UTC. `resolutionCriteria` defines the prospective binary event. `sourceUrls` lists cited background evidence, whose independent quality and relevance must be audited. `status: RES_OPEN` means no adjudication. `reviewGate` records the current evidence limitation.

Machine records are **snapshots**, not auto-updating feeds. A future official outcome requires a separate, documented review and should never overwrite the original question, cutoff, probability or recorded version.
