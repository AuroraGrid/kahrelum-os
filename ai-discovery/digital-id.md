# Digital Identity Watch

Canonical: https://kahrelum.com/digital-id

The KAHRELUM digital-identity watch distinguishes published legislation, administrative rollout, voluntary use, implementation announcements and unassessed jurisdictions. It does not establish a worldwide compulsory identification scheme.

## Published public record

- A national digital ID policy is not necessarily an enforced national biometric mandate.
- Source timestamps, local legal status, population impact and exemptions must be verified separately.
- Unassessed jurisdictions retain NOT ASSESSED rather than receiving invented adoption labels.
- This is public-source research, not legal advice, identity verification service or government system access.
