# KAHRELUM OS and Research Method

Canonical: https://kahrelum.com/os

The public method describes evidence intake, source classification, countercase development, uncertainty capture and audit records. A written methodology and synthetic example are not proof of a live deployed enterprise system.

## Published public record

- Freeze scope, information cutoff, permission boundary and decision criterion before analysing results.
- Record primary documents and contradictory testimony separately from inference, estimates or scenarios.
- Evaluate falsifiers, negative findings, unresolved gaps, and source provenance.
- Preserve original forecast probabilities and audit timestamps; do not backdate or falsely claim certification.

## Source and verification boundaries
