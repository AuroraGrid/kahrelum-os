# Decision Evidence Gap Scorecard | KAHRELUM

Canonical page: https://kahrelum.com/evidence-scorecard

A free local, browser-only worksheet to examine up to three historical AI decision records with eight PASS/GAP/UNKNOWN checks. It helps structure questions, not verify an organization or run an independent certification.

## What is publicly offered or reported

- Use appropriately de-identified and permitted historical decisions only. Do not enter secrets, credentials or customer data.
- Check evidence, source provenance, policy, authority and escalation controls; classify gaps honestly rather than forcing PASS.
- Export a local CSV for your own records. A displayed PASS is not an independent external certification.
- The paid $750 Fit Check and $2,500 Founding Pilot are separately scoped operator-assisted reviews, not features of this free tool.
