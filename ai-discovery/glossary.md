# KAHRELUM Evidence and Forecast Glossary

A concise, accessible public guide for people and research agents reading KAHRELUM. Terms below classify evidence, not an individual's guilt, market worth, or model superiority.

## Evidence labels

- **FACT:** A proposition supported by an explicitly named source in the reported time window. A filing verifies what the filer recorded, not necessarily the truth of disputed allegations in it.
- **ALLEGATION:** An attributed claim awaiting independent verification. Accusations about misconduct or intelligence ties must not be presented as adjudicated findings.
- **INFERENCE:** An analytical conclusion based on identified sources and assumptions, not a direct recorded observation.
- **FORECAST:** A probability or scenario issued before a stated settlement cutoff. Preserve the original probabilities, time, contrary evidence and exact resolution rules.
- **UNKNOWN:** Material evidence is absent, contradictory or outside the audited scope. A blank is not a positive finding.
- **PASS / GAP / UNKNOWN:** Evidence-to-decision control statuses in the free scorecard. These are reviewer classifications, not certifications of organisations.
- **FIT / NO FIT:** Commercial scoping output. NO FIT is a complete $750 Fit Check deliverable, not a failed purchase or claim of wrongdoing.

## Time and result boundaries

- **Publication date:** When an HTML page or summary was updated. It does not imply every original source was newly checked.
- **Source cutoff:** Last time the relevant material was actually inspected or admissible for a specific decision.
- **Official result / certification:** Jurisdiction-specific institutional process. Media calls or provisional vote counts are not necessarily state certification.
- **FC1:** A separately frozen NFL six-trade volume forecast. Its November 10 date-boundary reconciliation is unresolved and is not certified or scored.
- **C7:** The November 5, 2026 Israeli official-election-result calibration record. Its externally attributed comparator must not be described as original KAHRELUM forecasting superiority.
- **Brier score:** A proper probability forecast score where lower is better. The original 123-player NFL cohort result of 0.077813821138 was worse than an all-NO benchmark of 0.065040650407.
- **Synthetic:** Explicitly invented sample for teaching or demonstration, not an actual customer decision or an independently verified live run.

## Buyer and data-use boundaries

KAHRELUM's $750 Fit Check evaluates three authorised de-identified closed historical decisions. The $2,500 Founding Pilot covers up to ten decisions in one agreed workflow. Neither is a claim of accredited audit certification or live production access. The public inquiry prepares a message but does not send it. Do not include confidential records, credentials or customer identifiers in public fields.

## Source and verification boundaries

This glossary defines editorial labels rather than verifying an individual case. Always consult the linked source ledger, original cutoff and relevant official record before quoting a concrete claim. Research agents have read-only access to this public material and no implied permission to fetch customer data or amend published predictions.
