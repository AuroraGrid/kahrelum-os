# Prospective Validation Protocol — Public Outline

## Objective

Test KAHRELUM prospectively under conditions controlled by an external evaluator rather than by KAHRELUM's constructor.

## Preferred controls

The external evaluator controls:

1. case/question selection;
2. information cutoff and allowed evidence;
3. preregistration and frozen scoring rules;
4. resolution criteria and gold outcomes;
5. comparator inclusion and identity where applicable;
6. scoring and statistical analysis;
7. preservation/publication of positive and negative results.

## KAHRELUM commitments

- no post-hoc case deletion because of performance;
- no silent repair of frozen outputs;
- preserve blocks/refusals as outputs;
- separate point estimates from statistical support;
- preserve negative findings;
- disclose deviations from the registered protocol.

## Minimum outputs

Per case: frozen prompt/question, cutoff, KAHRELUM output, probability where required, evidence state, block/refusal state where applicable, resolution and score.

Aggregate: Brier or other preregistered proper score, calibration, paired differences where appropriate, uncertainty intervals, cost/runtime if measured, and failure-mode analysis.

## Interpretation

A prospective result is benchmark-specific evidence. It should not automatically be generalized into universal superiority.
