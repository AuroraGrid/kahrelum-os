SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE · PRERECORDED — NOT A LIVE KAHRELUM RUN
Not a live run. This is a prerecorded, explicitly synthetic example. Every scenario detail and value is invented for illustration. It was not generated by a fresh KAHRELUM execution and is not evidence about a real organization or project.

Question

Should a team commit now to an October 31 platform migration date?

Illustrative scenario only. All entities, dates, counts, and statements below are invented; none describe a real customer or project.

Evidence

  • stipulated synthetic fact: In this invented scenario, 8 of 10 planned service rehearsals have passed.
  • stipulated synthetic fact: The two remaining rehearsals cover the highest-dependency services.
  • stipulated synthetic fact: A rollback checklist exists, but no timed rollback rehearsal has been recorded.

Inference

Passing most rehearsals is encouraging, but the unresolved highest-dependency cases and untested rollback leave material readiness uncertainty. The evidence supports continued preparation, not a firm date commitment.

Uncertainty

  • The scenario provides no history showing how well rehearsal results predict production outcomes.
  • The scope, severity, and remediation time for the two remaining services are unspecified.
  • No independently verified rollback timing or recovery objective is provided.

Counterargument

The team could still meet the date: eight rehearsals passed, the remaining failures may be routine, and the checklist may be sufficient if a rollback is rarely needed. The scenario contains no evidence that the remaining work will miss the deadline.

Decision gate

HOLD DATE COMMITMENT

Commit only after both high-dependency rehearsals pass against the frozen acceptance criteria and a timed rollback rehearsal meets the agreed recovery objective. Reassess if either gate fails.

Record

Current disposition: continue preparation; do not treat the date as cleared by this example.

Record state: illustrative and prerecorded.
