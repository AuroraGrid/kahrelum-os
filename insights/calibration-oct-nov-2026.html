Gates, capital and operational exceptions

The internal audit reports 100% process and gate compliance (8/8) across the seven cases and one drill: the sports forecast was a HOLD / WATCH ONLY, $0-stake decision, and the synthetic drill blocked three prohibited/unverified actions. No real wager was made; this is zero exposure, not a demonstrated positive investment return.

Execution exception: the scheduled October 20 resolution check did not run autonomously and was later reconciled. The 8/8 gate metric must not be interpreted as 100% scheduler reliability. KAHRELUM OS v2.3.4 here is an instruction-layer Operational Beta, not proven frozen binary-runtime equivalence. No reported client investment returns, audited deployment SLA or superiority over competing forecasting organizations.
