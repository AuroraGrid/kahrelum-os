Known facts at our November 12 cutoff

Contemporary reporting quoted Beasley's attorney confirming a plea agreement. The announced December 9, 2026 appearance is a plea hearing, not a sentencing hearing. Public reporting does not establish a date for the eventual sentencing or disclose the final charges in his agreement. [2] [3]

In a different NBA gambling case, another defendant received a two-year prison sentence in January 2026. We treat it as weak context only, not as an interchangeable case or a forecast-guideline calculation. [4]

Why 62%?

Base case

Alleged participant role

Prosecutors allege direct player participation, potentially a more severe integrity offense than passive betting. The alleged conduct remains contested pending adjudication.

Mitigation

Unknown plea concessions

Accepted narrower offenses, acceptance of responsibility, cooperation credit and other mitigation could materially reduce imprisonment.

Blackglass countercase

A sentence under a year

The judge could impose probation or a short custodial term, especially if the count, loss assessment or cooperation differs from the charging narrative.

Statutory maximums for indicted crimes are not expected sentences. An individualized federal sentencing range requires plea facts, judge findings and a criminal-history calculation that are not yet public. [5]

Frozen resolution and scoring rules

YES: In E.D.N.Y. case 26-CR-190 (LDH), Beasley's first federal sentencing imposes 12 months or more of imprisonment. NO: The first sentence imposes less than 12 months, including zero, probation, fine or home detention without imprisonment.

PENDING: No sentence by December 31, 2028, or the initial sentencing record cannot be authenticated. Delay does not count as failure. VOID: The prosecution fully ends without a sentencing judgment; this does not count as a win.

Independent settlement: Verify the original judicial sentence through the federal court sentencing transcript, minute entry, written judgment or an official Justice Department sentencing statement. Do not substitute time actually served, the 2021 state case, an unaccepted plea or revised media speculation.

Binary Brier score: (0.62 − outcome)². A YES settles at 0.1444; NO at 0.3844. PENDING and VOID remain unscored. No deadline for a sentence is invented.
