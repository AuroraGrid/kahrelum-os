KAHRELUM Insights · OSINT literacy · Public intelligence

The OSINT illusion

The most convincing misinformation does not have to be entirely false. A real aviation record can make an unverified geopolitical narrative feel proven.

November 10, 2026 · Source review November 10 · Explainer, not a military forecast · Dated evidence ledger

Evidence boundary: The viral post discussed here came from a user-supplied screenshot, not an independently authenticated original URL. No flight tracker alone can establish cargo, passengers, covert orders or an imminent military strike. The purpose of this article is to explain how to distinguish observation from inference.

What is OSINT?

Open-source intelligence (OSINT) means collecting, verifying and analyzing information from publicly accessible or lawfully obtained sources: aviation records, satellite imagery, shipping data, company filings, government documents and photographs.

Technical detail conveys authority. A flight map can show an identifier, altitude, speed and position. But a technically precise display can be paired with an interpretation it never measured. Flightradar24 combines ADS-B signals, multilateration and other sources. The FAA describes ADS-B as transmitting aircraft position, altitude, speed and related tracking information. Those data are not a cargo manifest or secret mission order.

An authentic observation cannot automatically authenticate its storyteller.

Case study: heavy aircraft and the Pakistan warning

A viral November 2026 post displayed aircraft-tracking graphics and described heavy transport arrivals in Pakistan. It then claimed China had warned Russia about a possible U.S. attack on Iran from Nur Khan Air Base and invoked purported Israeli and Indian intelligence assessments.

Direct record · Limited finding

What the tracker shows

Flightradar24 identifies 7Q-VAF as an Ilyushin Il-76TD operated by Vise Air. Its visible flight history records an arrival in Karachi on November 6, 2026, with departures from Karachi on November 6 and November 8. Several other origin and destination fields remain “Not available.” This is evidence of provider-recorded movements, not of their purpose.

Unverified · Mission assertion

What the tracker cannot show

No verified evidence in this source review establishes the contents of cargo, passengers, that China delivered the alleged warning, that Israel or India confirmed it, or that the flights were connected to U.S. strike preparations at Nur Khan. Each allegation requires additional, independently attributable evidence.

November 6–8 movements do not automatically verify a later post's “last 24 hours” framing. Karachi and Nur Khan are different airports; observing one does not prove an operational mission at the other. “Unverified” is not the same thing as “disproved.”

How OSINT becomes a viral certainty trap

1 · Observation

The map is plausible

An aircraft identity or partial flight route may be checked with an original provider.

2 · Interpretation

The mission is imagined

Type, timing and geography suggest a purpose. This remains a hypothesis, not a verified finding.

3 · Attribution

Intelligence agencies are named

A precise registration number does not authenticate anonymous claims about secret government warnings.

4 · Story construction

Fragments connect into a plot

Separate rumors and observations become one narrative. Coherence is not independent corroboration.

5 · Viral repetition

Uncertainty disappears

Reposts keep the screenshot but lose the caveats. Ten posts quoting one unnamed source are not ten independent sources.

A six-question reader's checklist

  1. Is there an original record? Open the flight-tracker entry instead of relying on a crop.
  2. Does the timestamp match? Check the calendar date, time zone and missing route fields.
  3. What is observed versus inferred? Do not let an airport coordinate certify a covert mission.
  4. Are the sources independent? Trace repeated assertions to their first identifiable source.
  5. What alternatives fit? Commercial freight, military transport, maintenance and diplomatic logistics have different evidentiary signatures.
  6. What would change the answer? A trustworthy manifest, a primary official statement or corrected tracking evidence may justify revision.

The Berkeley Protocol on Digital Open Source Investigations, developed with the UN human rights office, details principles of responsible digital evidence collection, analysis and preservation.

Why intelligence must get updated

An honest investigation evolves when new evidence emerges. What should not evolve silently is the historical record: original observations, source cutoffs, rejected interpretations and corrections belong in the same visible audit trail.

KAHRELUM distinguishes verified observations supported by a record, corroborated findings independently supported by multiple sources, inferred explanations beyond direct observation, and unverified claims missing necessary evidence. The labels describe support for specific propositions, not the analyst's confidence in an entire dramatic story.

For the Karachi case, the listed Vise Air flights are provider-recorded facts. The alleged intelligence warnings and impending strike remain unverified in this review. Further evidence may change that assessment, and any such change should retain the original version and date of correction.

KAHRELUM principle: A radar screen can show where an aircraft traveled. It cannot tell you what its journey means.
