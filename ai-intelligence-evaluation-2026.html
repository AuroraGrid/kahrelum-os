Interpretation and limits

What the score does not prove. This is not a micro1 result, an external benchmark certification, model superiority, a client engagement, or evidence of operational intelligence accuracy. The scenarios and their expected answers were constructed together, and three case families are too small to estimate general reliability. Provider identity, run isolation and independent human grading have not been externally attested.

Primary sources, not invented incidents

Financial guidance: FinCEN's October 9, 2025 SAR FAQs (Q4). Collective defence: NATO's Article 5 guidance and cyber defence policy. Interview method: College of Policing guidance and National Academies deception research. These official documents are real; all case events are synthetic.

What would justify a stronger claim?

New unseen cases, independent adjudication, multiple real provider runs, model version and decoding settings, source-access logs and a preregistered scoring procedure. Until then, this result remains a transparent practice score.
